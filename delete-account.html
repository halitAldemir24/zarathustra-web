Delete Your Account

Permanently remove your Zarathustra account and all associated data.

Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data, including book analyses, generated images, character profiles, and chat history stored on our servers will be permanently deleted.

What Will Be Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed from our servers:

  • Account profile - Your email, display name, and account settings
  • Personalization data - Quiz responses and reading preferences
  • All book data - Book metadata, uploaded text chunks, and extraction data
  • AI analyses - Character profiles, event summaries, themes, and relationships
  • Generated images - All AI-generated character and event illustrations
  • Subscription records - Your subscription status and drop balance
  • Support requests - Any support tickets associated with your account

What Will NOT Be Deleted

  • Local data on your device - EPUB files, local reading progress, and cached data remain on your device. Uninstall the app to remove these.
  • Apple/Google purchase records - Transaction history remains with Apple or Google as per their policies.
  • Active subscription billing - If you have an active subscription, you must cancel it separately through your Apple or Google account settings. Deleting your Zarathustra account does not automatically cancel your subscription billing.

Important: Cancel Your Subscription First

If you have a premium subscription, please cancel it before deleting your account:

  • iOS: Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions > Zarathustra > Cancel
  • Android: Google Play Store > Menu > Subscriptions > Zarathustra > Cancel

How to Delete Your Account

Option 1: From Within the App (Recommended)

  1. Open Zarathustra and navigate to your Profile tab.
  2. Tap Account Settings to open your account preferences.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Delete Account" (shown in red).
  4. Confirm deletion when prompted. You may need to re-authenticate with your Google or Apple account.
  5. Your account and all server-side data will be permanently deleted immediately.

Option 2: Via Email Request

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion by email:

  1. Send an email to support.zarathustra@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
  2. Include your account email (the email address you used to sign in with Google or Apple).
  3. We will verify your identity and process the deletion within 72 hours.
  4. You will receive a confirmation email once your account has been deleted.

After Deletion

Once your account is deleted:

  • You will be signed out of the app immediately
  • You will not be able to recover your account or any associated data
  • If you wish to use Zarathustra again, you will need to create a new account
  • Any remaining drops or subscription benefits will be forfeited
