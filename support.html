Frequently Asked Questions

What is Zarathustra?

Zarathustra is an AI-powered e-reader that goes beyond simple reading. Upload any EPUB book, and our AI will analyze it to extract characters, themes, symbols, and narrative structure. You can then chat with Zarathustra about the book or even have conversations as characters from the story.

How does AI book analysis work?

When you request an analysis, the book text is sent to our secure servers where a multi-stage AI pipeline processes it. The pipeline includes a Scout (initial scan), Character Extractor, Engineer (detailed chunk analysis), Writer (summary generation), and Project Manager (final assembly). The entire process typically takes a few minutes depending on the book's length.

What book formats are supported?

Zarathustra currently supports EPUB format only. PDF and other formats are not supported at this time.

Is my book content private?

Yes. Your book content is only uploaded to our servers when you explicitly request an AI analysis. It is stored securely in your personal account space and is never shared with other users. You can delete your book data at any time. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

What are "drops"?

Drops are the in-app currency used to generate AI images of characters and events. Premium subscribers receive periodic drop refills. Each image generation costs a certain number of drops.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. To cancel:

  • iOS: Go to Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions > Zarathustra > Cancel
  • Android: Go to Google Play Store > Menu > Subscriptions > Zarathustra > Cancel

Your premium features will remain active until the end of your current billing period.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account from the app settings (Profile > Account Settings > Delete Account) or by visiting our Account Deletion page. Account deletion is permanent and removes all your data from our servers.

What languages are supported?

The Zarathustra app interface is available in English, Turkish, German, French, and Spanish. AI analysis and chat can process books in many languages, though results are best with widely-spoken languages.

Can I use Zarathustra offline?

You can read your books offline. However, AI features (analysis, chat, image generation) require an internet connection as they are processed on our servers.

The AI analysis is taking too long or seems stuck

Book analysis typically takes 2-5 minutes depending on the book's length and server load. If analysis appears stuck for more than 10 minutes, try closing and reopening the app. If the issue persists, please contact us.

I found an error in the AI analysis

AI-generated content may contain inaccuracies. If you notice significant errors, please let us know at support.zarathustra@gmail.com so we can improve our analysis quality.
