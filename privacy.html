Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 17, 2026

Zarathustra ("we", "our", or "the app") is an AI-powered e-reader application. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and what choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create an account using Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In, we receive and store:

  • Email address - Used for account identification
  • Display name - If provided by your sign-in provider
  • Profile photo URL - If provided by your sign-in provider

1.2 Personalization Data

If you complete the personalization quiz, we store your preferences:

  • Reading style (e.g., bookworm, mood reader)
  • Preferred genres (e.g., fantasy, mystery, romance)
  • Reading goals and schedule preferences
  • Reading pain points (e.g., forgetting characters)

1.3 Book Data

When you upload and analyze a book:

  • Book metadata - Title, author, chapter structure
  • Book text content - Uploaded to our servers only when you initiate AI analysis
  • AI-generated analysis - Character profiles, event summaries, themes, and relationships
  • AI-generated images - Character and event illustrations created upon your request

1.4 Chat & Query Data

When you ask questions to Zarathustra or chat as a character, your messages and conversation history are sent to our AI service to generate responses. Chat history is stored locally on your device.

1.5 Subscription Data

We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and in-app purchases. RevenueCat processes your purchase transactions through Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We store your subscription status, expiration date, and in-app currency balance ("drops").

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain the Zarathustra reading experience
  • Process AI book analysis, character extraction, and image generation
  • Respond to your questions and chat messages with AI-generated answers
  • Manage your subscription and in-app currency
  • Personalize your reading experience based on your quiz responses
  • Provide customer support

Important

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or selling to third parties. Your book content is used solely for generating AI analysis at your request.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate Zarathustra:

  • Firebase (Google) - Authentication, cloud database (Firestore), file storage, and cloud functions
  • OpenRouter - Routes AI requests to language models (Google Gemini, etc.) for book analysis and chat
  • fal.ai - AI image generation service (FLUX model) for character and event illustrations
  • RevenueCat - Subscription and in-app purchase management
  • Apple / Google - Sign-in authentication and payment processing

When you use AI features, portions of your book content and queries are sent to these services for processing. Each service is governed by their own privacy policies.

5. Data Storage & Security

5.1 Cloud Storage

Your account information, book analysis data, and generated images are stored on Firebase servers (Google Cloud Platform). Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

5.2 Local Storage

Your EPUB files, reading progress, chat history, and cached images are stored locally on your device. This data is not uploaded to our servers unless you explicitly initiate an AI analysis.

5.3 Security Measures

  • Firebase App Check protects against unauthorized API access
  • Firestore security rules ensure users can only access their own data
  • Subscription and currency data can only be modified by our servers
  • All data transmission uses HTTPS encryption

6. Data Retention & Deletion

6.1 While Your Account Is Active

We retain your data as long as your account is active. You can delete individual book analyses at any time through the app.

6.2 Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the app settings or by visiting our Account Deletion page. When you delete your account:

  • Your user profile and all associated data are permanently removed from our servers
  • All book analyses, generated images, and chat data are deleted
  • Your subscription (if active) will remain valid until the end of the billing period but will not renew
  • Local data on your device is not automatically deleted — you can uninstall the app to remove it

7. Children's Privacy

Zarathustra is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected data from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict processing of your data
  • Data portability

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the email address listed below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
